## Medical/educational boundary
ECG P wave precedes mechanical atrial contraction, QRS electrical event precedes ventricular pressure rise, and normalized Wiggers pressures/volume are illustrations, not patient measurements or evidence of clinical precision. The two model filling windows are not claims about physiological diastolic filling rate. See preexisting V1.12_CLINICAL_CURRICULUM.md and V1.12_PATCH5_REPORT.md for source and citation discussion.

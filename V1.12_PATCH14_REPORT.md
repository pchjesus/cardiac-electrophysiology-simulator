## Risk assessment and solution
1. Viewport-only 900px switch could still squeeze columns when actual content area is narrower due to zoom or container layout. Keep the accepted desktop two-column display but use auto-fit with 320px cell minima and automatic fallback to stacked content; narrow screens remain stacked.
2. Absolutely-positioned details body could overlay neighboring content. On expansion the original native details stays in the readout's normal flow; closed UI remains compact.
3. Core Mechanical live readouts were 12–13px. Raise phase explanations to 14px and stats to 13px; native disclosure targets to >=40px on PC and >=44px on mobile.
4. Correct stale SPEC current version pointer. Keep model limitations and guided assumptions accessible on demand.
